{%- comment -%} Publications. The head: the shell's title, the publication summary (_includes/pub-summary.html; the journals with their counts, then the conference papers and theses), and the year index (the papers' years, then theses_heading). One section per year of the papers (every type but thesis), newest first; within a year month descending (no month counts as 0), ties in file order. Then the theses, ordered the same way (T2, T1). Each entry one pub-row.html row with its label on its own line.
