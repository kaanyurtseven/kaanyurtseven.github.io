Research focus

Full research overview

  • 01

    Congestion management and balancing under uncertainty

    Coordinated optimization of congestion management and balancing decisions, explicitly accounting for their coupling to reduce operational costs and risk.

  • 02

    Quantifying the impact of uncertainty on decisions

    Analysis of how probabilistic forecasts influence operational decisions and system costs, providing transparency on how uncertainty shapes outcomes.

  • 03

    Risk-aware optimization under non-Gaussian uncertainty

    Development of stochastic optimization frameworks that capture realistic uncertainty distributions, enabling informed and economically efficient system operation.
