    <section class="cv-section">
      <h2 class="cv-section__title">Research profile</h2>
      <div class="prose" style="margin-top: 0.5rem;">
        <p>
          My work focuses on stochastic optimization and uncertainty-aware operational planning
          in renewable-dominated power systems. In particular, I develop frameworks that quantify
          how uncertainty affects congestion management and balancing decisions, enabling more
          cost-efficient and reliable grid operation.
        </p>
        <p>
          I work on continuous, non-Gaussian uncertainty modeling using Polynomial Chaos Expansion
          and risk-based formulations, bridging academic research with practical challenges faced
          by transmission system operators.
        </p>
      </div>
    </section>
